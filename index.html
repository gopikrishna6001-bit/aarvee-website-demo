Milestones

From a home office to a public engineering company.

The practice’s own account of how it grew, plus the corporate steps now on the public record.

  1. 1989Founded in Hyderabad by three people, working from a home office.
  2. 1998Entered highways as India’s road programme scaled up.
  3. 2000Graduate Training Program began — still how new engineers learn the practice.
  4. 2002Irrigation, railways and GIS joined the work.
  5. 2006Ports. Coastal logistics became a sector, not a side brief.
  6. 2008Metro rail, as Indian cities started building urban transit.
  7. 2011Australia. The first office outside India.
  8. 2022United Kingdom. Partnerships in rail, power and water.
  9. 2025Became Aarvee Engineering Consultants Limited, a public company. FY25 revenue ₹567 crore, profit ₹51 crore.
  10. 2026SEBI cleared the path for an IPO of up to ₹202.5 crore in fresh shares. Environmental clearance for the Narayanpet–Kodangal lift irrigation scheme: 40,470 hectares, 123 villages.

Journey years are Aarvee’s published history. The 2025 figures and 2026 IPO and clearance notes are from the company’s draft offer document, as reported by The Times of India, and from Aarvee’s own announcement.

Life at Aarvee

A practice people join to do the real work.

More than 4,000 engineers, planners and designers. The culture the firm publishes is project-first: commitment, reliability, quality, and delivery on time. The graduate programme, running since 2000, is how students become site-ready engineers.

Founder R. V. Chakrapani puts it this way: civil engineering is a responsibility — stay disciplined, stay curious, and do it the right way, not the easy way.
